Why SQGATE is the Superior Modern Alternative to Cedar Logic Simulator (Cedar LS)

For decades, Cedar Logic Simulator (Cedar LS) was a staple in university computer science and electrical engineering departments. Developed as an open-source academic tool, Cedar LS introduced thousands of students to digital logic concepts, registers, and basic CPU architecture. However, Cedar LS has not seen active development in years. As an aging, Windows-only desktop application, it creates steep barriers for students on macOS, Linux, and Chromebooks, frequently suffering from rendering glitches, high-DPI scaling bugs, and installation headaches on modern 64-bit systems.

SQGATE represents the next generation of digital circuit simulation. Built from the ground up on modern web technologies, SQGATE is 100% web-based and requires zero installation. Whether you are working on a MacBook, a university Chromebook, an Ubuntu workstation, or a Windows 11 desktop, SQGATE launches instantly in any browser. It delivers a fluid 60 FPS hardware-accelerated dark-mode interface with smooth infinite canvas panning, pinch-to-zoom, and responsive snap-to-grid alignment that makes Cedar LS feel archaic.

In addition to cross-platform accessibility, SQGATE bridges the critical divide between schematic capture and physical hardware implementation. While Cedar LS confines designs to a proprietary desktop sandbox, SQGATE supports FPGAs via automated structural Verilog export. With one click, your graphical schematic—from multiplexers to multi-bit counters—is converted into synthetically valid Verilog HDL. You can directly import this code into industry-standard FPGA toolchains like AMD Xilinx Vivado or Intel Quartus Prime to run your logic on real silicon.

SQGATE also enriches the learning experience with integrated pedagogical tools that Cedar LS lacks entirely. Designers have instant access to an interactive Karnaugh Map (K-Map) solver up to 6 variables, an automated truth table generator, and a dedicated Finite State Machine (FSM) designer for Mealy and Moore models. Circuits are saved securely in your browser's IndexedDB with offline Progressive Web App (PWA) support, giving you total privacy and instant access anywhere.
