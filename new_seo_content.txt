      <h2 style="color: var(--t1); font-size: 24px; margin-bottom: 20px; border-top: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1); padding-top: 32px;">How to Use the SQGATE Circuit Simulator</h2>
      
      <h3 style="color: #fff; font-size: 18px; margin-bottom: 12px;">Step 1: Access the Components Library</h3>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">Begin your design by opening the left-hand Library panel. Here you will find a categorized list of components ranging from basic logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, XOR) to advanced sequential elements like D Flip-Flops, T Flip-Flops, and J-K Flip-Flops. Simply click and drag any component from the library onto the grid canvas. You can use the search bar at the top of the library to quickly locate specific chips or I/O elements like LED matrices and hexadecimal displays.</p>

      <h3 style="color: #fff; font-size: 18px; margin-bottom: 12px;">Step 2: Connect Wires and Route Signals</h3>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">Wiring in SQGATE is designed to be completely fluid. Hover over any output pin (indicated by a small circular node) until it highlights, then click and drag your cursor to the desired input pin on another gate. The simulator automatically routes the wire orthogonally, minimizing wire clutter and crossed paths. To delete a wire, hover over it until it highlights and press the Delete or Backspace key. Signal states are color-coded in real time: bright cyan represents a logical HIGH (1), while subtle dark blue represents a logical LOW (0).</p>

      <h3 style="color: #fff; font-size: 18px; margin-bottom: 12px;">Step 3: Simulate and Verify Logic</h3>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">Simulation is always live. To test your circuit, place an Input Toggle or Push Button from the library and connect it to your logic path. Click the switches to toggle their state and watch the signals propagate through your gates instantly. For sequential circuits, you can add a Clock generator and adjust its frequency via the right-hand Properties panel. Use the automated Truth Table Generator tool to instantly verify the complete input-output mapping of your combinational blocks.</p>

      <h3 style="color: #fff; font-size: 18px; margin-bottom: 12px;">Step 4: Export to Verilog and Save</h3>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 32px;">Once your design is functionally correct, you can export it for use in FPGA toolchains. Remember to click "Save" to store your project locally in your browser's offline workspace, or generate a shareable link to collaborate with classmates.</p>
